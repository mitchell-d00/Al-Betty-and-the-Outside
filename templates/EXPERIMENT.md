# Experiment plan

- **ID and question** — What uncertainty should this test resolve?
- **Maintainer direction** — Who authorized which revision and scope? Which proposed actions still await a decision?
- **Baseline and candidate** — Give the exact baseline and the candidate criteria. Add the candidate pin before testing.
- **Frozen inputs** — List stable IDs, positive cases, contrasting controls, preservation cases, hashes, and allowed distributions.
- **Expected decisions** — Record each case’s intended result and the condition that would falsify the claim.
- **Execution and scoring** — Record the commands, environment, limits, scorer version, denominators, and how failures or missing runs count.
- **Custody and exposure** — Name the preparer, runner, scorer, and custodian. Record who saw the cases and answers and when.
- **Results and receipts** — Link baseline and candidate raw outputs, attempts, skips, and blockers. Keep scores under their original fixtures and rules.
- **Decision** — State what passed within scope, what remains open, and who decides the next action.
